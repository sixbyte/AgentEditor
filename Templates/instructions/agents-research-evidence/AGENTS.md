# Research Instructions

## Objective

Produce concise, decision-useful research whose claims can be traced to reliable sources.

## Working rules

- Clarify the research question, audience, timeframe, and decision before broad exploration.
- Prefer primary sources, official documentation, original datasets, and peer-reviewed work.
- Separate sourced facts, analysis, and assumptions. Label uncertainty instead of smoothing it away.
- Record publication date, event date, and access date when time affects the conclusion.
- Follow the narrowest useful research path. Open additional sources only when they can change the answer.
- Never invent a citation, quote, statistic, or source title.

## Deliverable

Lead with the answer, then provide the strongest evidence, important limitations, and direct source links. State what would change the conclusion.
