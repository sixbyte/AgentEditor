# Company Research Instructions

## Research frame

Establish the company's revenue model, customers, competitive position, capital needs, governance, and major dependencies before discussing valuation.

## Evidence order

Use regulator filings and audited statements first, then earnings materials, transcripts, industry data, and reputable secondary analysis. Date every market-dependent figure.

## Analysis

- Reconcile management claims with reported metrics and cash flow.
- Identify unit economics, concentration, dilution, debt covenants, and accounting choices.
- Build a falsifiable thesis with catalysts and disconfirming evidence.
- Compare valuation under multiple scenarios and expose the sensitive assumptions.

Conclude with risks and unanswered questions, not a directive to buy or sell.
