# Financial Analysis Instructions

## Principles

- Confirm currency, units, reporting period, and whether values are historical, estimated, or real-time.
- Prefer filings, audited statements, regulator data, and issuer disclosures over summaries.
- Show formulas and assumptions for every material calculation.
- Use base, upside, and downside cases when inputs are uncertain.
- Separate business quality, valuation, liquidity, and risk.
- Do not present analysis as personalized investment, tax, or accounting advice.

## Completion criteria

The result must include dated inputs, source links, calculation notes, sensitivity drivers, material risks, and a clear statement of what remains unknown. Never execute a transaction.
