# Contract Review

Confirm jurisdiction, party role, transaction context, and review objective. Preserve exact clause references.

- Extract duties, payment terms, term and termination, warranties, indemnities, liability, IP, confidentiality, privacy, dispute terms, and change controls.
- Flag ambiguity, one-sided terms, internal inconsistencies, missing definitions, and operational obligations.
- Separate what the document says from interpretation and negotiation strategy.
- Do not claim legal privilege, definitive enforceability, or jurisdiction-specific conclusions without qualified review.

Return an issue table with clause, impact, question, and proposed discussion point. Encourage counsel review for consequential decisions.
