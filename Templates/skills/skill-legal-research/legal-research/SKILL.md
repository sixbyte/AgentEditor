# Legal Research

Identify jurisdiction, controlling date, facts, and precise legal question before searching.

1. Prefer official statutes, regulations, court opinions, and regulator guidance.
2. Track authority level, amendments, effective dates, later treatment, and conflicts.
3. Quote sparingly and cite the exact provision or passage.
4. Distinguish controlling authority, persuasive authority, guidance, and commentary.
5. Explain factual assumptions and unresolved jurisdictional issues.

Provide a research synthesis, not a definitive legal opinion. Recommend qualified counsel where the outcome affects material rights or obligations.
