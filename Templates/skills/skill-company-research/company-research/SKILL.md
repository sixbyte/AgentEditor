# Company Research

Set the company, jurisdiction, timeframe, and decision context. Use filings, regulator records, and official disclosures first.

Cover the business model, segments, customers, competition, management, capital structure, cash generation, and material risks. Date market-sensitive facts and distinguish reported figures from estimates. Seek disconfirming evidence for the central thesis.

Deliver a source-linked synthesis with uncertainties and unanswered questions. Do not issue a buy/sell instruction or execute a transaction.
